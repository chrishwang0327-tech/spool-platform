Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

This policy explains what information SPOOL (Noboundary Apparel Inc.) collects at spoolnyc.com, how we use it, and your choices. We don't sell your personal information.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and sign-in records.
  • Your designs: images and references you upload, notes, generated designs, saved projects and tech packs.
  • Sample requests: name, brand, email, phone, shipping city/state/ZIP/country, sizes, quantities, target price and notes you enter.
  • Usage: pages viewed and actions such as creating, saving or requesting a sample, with a random browser ID; basic device and browser data; the site that referred you.
  • Messages: what you send us by email or live chat.

How we use it

  • To run the Service: sign you in, generate and save designs, and show them to you on any device.
  • To quote and produce samples and orders, including sharing design files and specs with the factories that make them.
  • To answer your questions and send emails about your account and requests.
  • To understand how the Service is used, fix problems, prevent abuse and enforce usage limits.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run SPOOL. They process data on our behalf: Supabase (accounts, database and file storage), Cloudflare (hosting, security, email forwarding), OpenAI and Google (AI image generation — your references and prompts are sent to them to create your designs), Resend (sending emails), and our live chat provider. Manufacturing partners receive design files and specs only when you request a sample or production.

Browser storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in, remember drafts and recent designs, and store a random ID for usage counts. We don't use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and designs while your account is active. Sample request records are kept as business records. You can ask us to delete your account and designs at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can request a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by emailing info@spoolnyc.com. Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have additional rights, including to know what we collect and to opt out of sales or sharing — we don't sell or share personal information for advertising.

Security

Your files are stored privately and are only accessible to your account and our team. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your own copies of important work.

Children

SPOOL is not for children under 13, and accounts are for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We'll update the effective date when this policy changes, and let you know about material changes.
