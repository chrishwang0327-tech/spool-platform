From reference to sample.

Four steps. Front and back are designed together.

01

Upload references

Graphic, body, wash and distress — a slot for each, front and back, with a note for each.

02

Create

Two designs per Create, each with a front and a back. Draft for cheap ideas, Final for sharper images.

03

Edit each part

AI isn't perfect. Fix the graphic, body, wash, distress or details separately — the rest stays as is.

04
