How sampling works

Questions.

Anything else? Tap the chat button — we reply as soon as we can.

Is it free to try?

Yes. Every account gets 10 free designs, the full editor, image downloads and your first tech pack free. SPOOL Studio ($29/month) unlocks unlimited designs, tech packs and production files.

How much does a sample cost?

It depends on the garment and techniques. Send a sample request and we'll reply with a quote within 1–2 business days. Nothing is charged until you accept.

What's the minimum for production?

We quote production after your sample is approved, based on your design, techniques and quantities.

Do I get print-ready files?

SPOOL Studio members can download vector SVG artwork at print size for flat-color graphics. With a sample, SPOOL prepares the full production artwork — vector files, color separations and embroidery files. Have your own logo file? Add it to your sample request so we match it exactly.

Can I cancel SPOOL Studio?

Anytime, from the account menu → Manage billing. You keep Studio until the end of the month you paid for.

Who owns the design?

You do. We use your files only to make your samples and orders. See our Terms.

Where do you ship?

We currently ship samples within the United States. Ask us in chat about other countries.
