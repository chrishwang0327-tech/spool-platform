Questions.

Anything else? Tap the chat button — we reply as soon as we can.

Is it free to try?

Yes. Every account gets 10 free designs in the Design Studio. Editing, image downloads and tech packs are free. Production files come with your sample.

How much does a sample cost?

It depends on the garment and techniques. Send a sample request and we'll reply with a quote within 1–2 business days. Nothing is charged until you accept.

What's the minimum for production?

We quote production after your sample is approved, based on your design, techniques and quantities.

Do I get print-ready files?

Production artwork — vector files, color separations and embroidery files — is prepared by SPOOL and included with your sample. Have your own logo file? Add it to your sample request so we match it exactly.

Who owns the design?

You do. We use your files only to make your samples and orders. See our Terms.

Where do you ship?

We currently ship samples within the United States. Ask us in chat about other countries.
